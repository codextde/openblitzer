Note on use

Under § 23 (1c) of the German road traffic regulations (StVO), anyone driving a vehicle must not operate or carry in a ready-to-use state a technical device intended to show or interfere with traffic enforcement. This includes apps on a phone. OpenBlitzer is meant for passengers and for planning routes. Other countries have their own rules; the app is not offered in Switzerland.
